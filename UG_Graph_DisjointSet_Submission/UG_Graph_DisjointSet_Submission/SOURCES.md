# Source and provenance note

The dataset is map-informed rather than a claim of official surveyed road measurements.

1. University of Ghana, "Welcome to UG Legon Campus" — confirms Legon Campus and the five traditional halls and describes campus facilities.
2. University of Ghana, 2026 Matriculation Ceremony document — describes the Main University Gate, University Avenue, Commonwealth Hall, J.B. Danquah Avenue, University Square and Balme Library relationship.
3. University of Ghana RSGIS Lab, "University of Ghana Campus Map" — used to confirm the names and relative placement of roads, academic buildings, halls, services and sports facilities.
4. Radio Univers, "Finding your way around the streets of Legon" — used as a secondary local-layout check for JQB, KAB, LECIAD, N Block, K Folson, Central Cafeteria, SRC, departments and halls.
5. Cartogiraffe map pages — used only as a secondary proximity sanity check for several roads/buildings around Dr. J.B. Danquah Avenue.

Important: the public sources do not provide an official surveyed distance for every selected graph edge. Therefore, the distance values in `roads.csv` are labelled as constructed/estimated walking-route distances for the DSA experiment. Do not call them official UG measurements.
